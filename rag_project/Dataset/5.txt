In the year one hundred and three-103-, at the auspicious moment of the constellation of Rohini, on the fifth tithi of the bright fortnight of Vaiśākha, (during the reign) of Rajan Kshatrapa Svāmi Rudrasimha, son of Rajan Mahākshatrapa Svāmi Rudradaman, grandson of Rajan Kshatrapa Svāmi Jayadaman and great-grandson of Rājan Mahākshatrapa Svāmi Chashțana, Senapati Rudrabhūti, son of Abhira Senäpati Bapaka, caused (this) step-well to be dug and to be built for the well-being and happiness of all creatures.
