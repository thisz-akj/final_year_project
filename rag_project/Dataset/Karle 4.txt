Success! In the twenty-fourth-24-year of the King Väsishthiputra illustrious Puļumāvi, in the third-3- fortnight of winter on the second-2-day, this pious gift of a mandapa (vihāra) of nine cells was given to the Universal Sangha as the special property of the Mahasanghikas by the Upāsaka Harapharaņa, the son of Setapharana, who belongs to the Sauvarshakato (or Kāśyapiya) Sangha and is a resident of Abulāmā, in honour of his father and mother and for securing the well-being and happiness of all creatures. It had been excavated in the twenty-first (regnal) year. Buddharakshita and his Upāsikā mother have rendered assistance to me. There is another pious gift of the mother of Buddharakshita behind (this Cave)
