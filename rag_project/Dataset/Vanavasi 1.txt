In the year 12 of the century-long reign of King Häritiputra Vishņukada Chuțukulānanda Satakarni, in the fortnight 7 of winter (and) on the day 1-on this day... who is Mahābhuvi (Mahabhoji), the daughter of a Mahārāja, the blessed mother of the Yuvarāja Šiva Skanda Nāgasri, caused a Naga to be engraved, a tank to be dug, and a vihara (monastery) to be constructed. Amatya Skanda Sati was the Supervisor of these works. Nataka, the pupil of the Acharya Indramayūraka of Sajayata, engraved this Nāga.
