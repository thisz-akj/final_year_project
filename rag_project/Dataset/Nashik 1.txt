From the victorious camp of the triumphant army in Govardhana, the illustrious Gautamiputra Satakarni, the lord of Benākaṭaka", issues the following) order to Amatya Vishnupālita in Govardhana: The field of two hundred 200 nivartanas (known as that) of Ajakālaka (yaksha) in the village of Western Kakhadi, which was (previously) owned by Rishabhadatta-that our field of two hundred- 200 nivartanas We give to these mendicant monks of the Triraśmi (Caves) and We (also) grant them the following immunities of that field (viz.) it must not be entered (by any royal servant); it must not be interfered with; it must not be dug for (obtaining) salt; it must not be meddled with by the district functionaries; it should be provided with all immunities. You should register (this) field and its immunities. This order has been given orally (by Us). It has been written down by the Amatya Sivagupta. It has been safely preserved by the Mahāsvamikas. Its patikā was given (on the following date)-the year 18, the rainy season fortnight 2, (and) the day 1. (It was) prepared by Tāpasa
