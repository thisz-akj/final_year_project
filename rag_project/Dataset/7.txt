In the year 127, on the 5th tithi of the dark fortnight of Bhadrapada (during the reign) of Rajan Mahākshatrapa Svāmi Rudrasena, (who is) great-great- grandson of a son of Rajan Mahākshatrapa Bhadra- mukha Svāmi Chashtana, great-grandson of a son of Rajan Kshatrapa Svami Jayadaman, grandson of Rajan, Mahākshatrapa Bhadramukha. Svāmi Rudradaman, (and) son of Rajan Mahakshatrapa Bhadramukha Svami Rudrasimha, this sutra has been set up by the brothers of Khararpattha, son of Pratāsaka, of the Manasa gotra. to heaven!
